Top alternatives to TrustMRR for tracking and publicly verifying startup monthly recurring revenue (MRR) include ShowMRR, VerifiedMRR (optimized for Indian payment rails like Razorpay), OpenRevenue, StartuPage, ProvenMRR, and analytics-heavy options like Baremetrics or ChartMogul.Public Leaderboards & Revenue VerificationShowMRR & ProvenMRR: Direct alternatives that list and rank bootstrapping startups by verified Stripe or paddle-backed revenue data.VerifiedMRR: Tailored specifically for the Indian SaaS ecosystem with native integrations for Razorpay and Dodo Payments instead of US-only billing rails.StartuPage: Connects to Stripe to verify numbers and turns your metrics into founder profiles, leaderboards, and investor matching.OpenRevenue: An open-source and self-hosted alternative that lets you verify and display your revenue transparently on your own infrastructure.Internal Metrics & Analytics AlternativesBaremetrics: Provides deep financial metrics, customer insights, and subscription forecasting beyond a simple public badge.ChartMogul: Focuses heavily on subscription data analytics, cohort analysis, and MRR tracking for internal growth rather than public flexing.
