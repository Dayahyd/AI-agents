These four names are all connected to the world of training Artificial Intelligence (AI), but they represent different companies and projects within that industry. Here's a breakdown of what each one is.

📚 iMerit Scholars
This is an elite program run by the company iMerit . It's not a gig-style platform but a curated community of highly skilled experts—like people with PhDs, MDs, or lawyers—who are brought in to work on advanced AI projects . These "Scholars" use their deep knowledge to perform complex tasks like fine-tuning models, creating specialized training data, and ensuring AI outputs are accurate and unbiased . Think of it as a high-end service for companies that need expert-level human input to make their AI truly specialized .

💻 Outlier
Outlier is an AI training platform owned by the larger company Scale AI . It connects a large, global network of freelance experts (over 100,000) with remote projects to improve AI models . These experts work on a wide variety of tasks, from reviewing AI-generated text for accuracy to solving math problems and writing code to help the models learn and reason better . Unlike the iMerit Scholars program, Outlier is more of an open platform where freelancers with skills in over 40 different domains can sign up and work flexibly .

🤖 Micro1
Micro1 is a separate AI startup that acts like a talent and data infrastructure company for AI labs . Its primary role is to use AI to recruit and manage other human experts for AI training projects . They use an AI recruiter named "Zara" to screen and vet candidates from around the world, building a database of experts to supply to companies that need human intelligence to train their models . It's less a training platform you work directly on and more of a high-tech staffing agency for the AI industry .

🥭 Multimango
This was an internal project name for a specific type of work on the Outlier platform (and possibly others) . It was a generalist project that involved tasks like text generation and evaluating conversations (RLHF) and did not require specialized degrees. It's important to note that Project Multimango ended in late 2025, so it's no longer available for new work . The project's shutdown was a standard occurrence in this industry, as funding for specific training runs eventually concludes .

In short, iMerit Scholars, Outlier, and Micro1 are all different players in the AI training ecosystem, while Multimango was a now-defunct project on the Outlier platform.

🏢 Other Major Players in the AI Data Ecosystem
These companies represent the broader landscape that platforms like Micro1 and services like Multimango are part of, covering everything from data annotation to data marketplaces.

Scale AI & Appen: These are major, established companies offering large-scale data annotation and labeling services. If you provide the raw data, they label it. Outlier is actually owned by Scale AI, connecting it directly to one of the biggest names in the industry.

Labelbox: A well-known annotation platform. Instead of being a service like Scale AI, they provide software that your own team can use to label data for images, video, text, and more.

Surge AI: A specialist in high-quality human feedback and RLHF, similar to what Outlier offers but often positioned as a premium service.

Protege: A platform that helps create partnerships between rights holders and AI labs, focusing on ethically sourcing specialized training data.

Troveo, Defined.ai, Veritone: These companies operate as licensed data marketplaces. They sell rights-cleared, pre-collected datasets (like video, audio, or images) to AI developers who need high-quality training data, circumventing legal issues that can arise from web scraping. This fits the data supply side of the AI industry that Micro1 helps staff.
